AirplayAtTheCrib for Mac — no building or Terminal needed.

Download **AirplayAtTheCrib.zip**, unzip it, and drag **AirplayAtTheCrib.app** into Applications.

On first launch, macOS may block the app because this release is not notarized. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**, then confirm **Open**. Allow **Local Network** access if asked.

Click **Find TVs**, choose your Roku, and click **Show in AirPlay**. Then open **Control Center → Screen Mirroring** and select the TV. Keep the app running while casting.

Supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 13 or later. Each Mac needs its own copy. This does not fix discovery on iPhones or bypass a network that blocks direct TV connections.

Early release: discovery and real-device AirPlay record retrieval are tested; successful end-to-end video mirroring has not yet been verified.
